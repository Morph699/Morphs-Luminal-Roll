Luminal Roll: Kinetic Fog Rush

Luminal Roll is a fast-paced 3D arcade rolling game built with WebGL and Three.js. You pilot a luminous, kinetic sphere along an endless neon highway suspended in the sky, dodging hazards, collecting power-ups, triggering bullet-time speed controls, and blasting obstacles off the track.
Game Modes
Classic Run
Endless high-speed track navigation.
Dodge rotating barriers, jump over laser fences, and thread through pylons as velocity steadily ramps up.
Kinetic Pusher (Switchball-Inspired Mode)
A physics-heavy mode focused on clearing the road.
Ram through or shoot movable 3D geometric obstacles (cubes, cylinders, polyhedral boulders, pyramids, and wedges) to knock them tumbling into the abyss.
Features a dedicated firing mechanic: click Left Mouse Button (LMB) or press F/Enter to shoot high-velocity plasma bolts directly ahead to blast blocks out of your path.
Velocity Control
High-throttle running with an interactive Slow-Motion / Bullet-Time gauge.
Hold S (or tap the Slow-Mo button on mobile) to dilate time and weave through dense laser gauntlets with surgical precision.
Power-Ups & Mechanics
Hyper Boost (Cyan): Surges the ball forward with warp-speed streaks and invincible ramming power.
Phase Shield (Green): Envelops the sphere in a protective energy barrier that absorbs lethal collisions.
Prism Magnet (Purple): Attracts distant score prisms and energy pickups.
Combo Multipliers (Amber): Chains pickups and block clears to elevate your multiplier up to 8×.
Nitro Energy Gauge: Accumulates energy passively and through pick-ups; trigger for instant speed boosts.
Visuals & Audio
Atmospheric 3D Skybox & Volumetric Fog: Drifting clouds, deep blues, and dynamic lighting with responsive surface shadows and ball wake effects.
Themes: Multiple visual colorways (Sky Blue with clouds, Neon Cyberpunk, Synthwave Sunset, Void Abyss).
Adaptive Sound Engine: Procedurally synthesized rolling hum that shifts pitch with speed and airtime, accompanied by laser fire chirps and heavy physical collision crunches.
Control Schemes
Mouse: Steer by moving your cursor horizontally · LMB: Shoot blocks (in Kinetic mode) or Jump · RMB: Nitro boost.
Keyboard: A / D or ← / → to steer · W / S to accelerate or brake/slow-mo · Space: Jump (in all modes) · F / Enter: Shoot plasma bolts · Shift / E: Nitro.
Mobile & Touch: Four selectable control styles:
Swipe Drag: Snappy, finger-following lateral steering.
Split Screen: Tap or hold left/right screen halves.
Virtual Joystick: Analog floating thumb stick.
Device Tilt: Gyroscope motion steering with a one-tap neutral calibration button.
On-screen buttons for Shoot, Jump, and Nitro, placed to keep your view of the track completely unobstructed.
